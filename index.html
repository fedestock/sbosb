Sustainable and healty travelin

At San Blas on Sailboats, we sail in harmony with the ocean, respecting nature, cultivating wellness, and creating authentic connections with the Kuna people.

Each journey is designed to care for the environment and ensure that every trip leaves only footprints in the heart, not on the land.

Responsible Anchoring & Snorkeling

We drop anchor only on sandy areas to protect coral reefs. Guests are guided to explore responsibly: avoid touching coral, use reef-safe sunscreen, and leave nature untouched.

Plastic-Free Living Onboard

We eliminate single-use plastics, replacing them with biodegradable and reusable products. Drinking water is filtered onboard, allowing guests to refill bottles safely.

Eco-Friendly Cleaning

All cleaning products are biodegradable, and we use natural sponges and compostable tools to avoid harmful chemicals.

Nourishing both body and soul is at the heart of our sailing experience. Every meal reflects sustainability, health, and the vibrant local flavors of Kuna Yala.

Fresh & Local Ingredients

We cook with organic fruits and vegetables, freshly caught fish from Kuna fishermen, and wholesome staples like legumes, oats, nuts, and tropical fruits.

Dietary Options

We cater to all diets, with vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free menus available. Each dish is balanced, nourishing, and full of flavor.

Sustainable Kitchen

By sourcing ingredients locally and minimizing imports, we reduce waste and support the island communities. Meals are served with natural, eco-friendly materials, with zero plastic waste.

We accept payments via PayPal and Wise. A 20% deposit is required to confirm your booking.

Since we live on remote islands far from banks or payment centers, and many local services only accept cash, the remaining payment must be made in cash either upon arrival or on the boat. This ensures we can cover all local expenses safely and smoothly.

Our crew consists of a captain and a sailor/cook. Life on a sailboat is very different from hotels or cruises it’s more like a motorhome on the water. We move with both sails and a motor, use solar energy, and have limited fresh water for showers (drinking water is always available). Spaces are minimalist but well organized, and most of the time we wear swimsuits and go barefoot.

Activities are personalized: snorkeling, island exploration, and time with guests, while also allowing moments of solitude. Transfers to the sailboat and the dinghy require some physical effort, so basic swimming and water skills are needed.

We minimize plastic use and ask guests to bring coral-friendly sunscreen.

Our prices reflect the high costs of maintaining the sailboat, taxes, and the care we put into providing a safe and memorable adventure.

If Lady Lou, our flagship sailboat, is already booked, we work with a carefully selected range of sailboats and catamarans in San Blas. We recommend these boats because their crews are kind, professional, and offer a service very similar to ours. Each option is personally selected to ensure a high-quality and authentic experience.

Our crew consists of a captain and a sailor/cook. Life on a sailboat is very different from hotels or cruises it’s more like a motorhome on the water. We move with both sails and a motor, use solar energy, and have limited fresh water for showers (drinking water is always available). Spaces are minimalist but well organized, and most of the time we wear swimsuits and go barefoot.

Activities are personalized: snorkeling, island exploration, and time with guests, while also allowing moments of solitude. Transfers to the sailboat and the dinghy require some physical effort, so basic swimming and water skills are needed.

We minimize plastic use and ask guests to bring coral-friendly sunscreen.

Our prices reflect the high costs of maintaining the sailboat, taxes, and the care we put into providing a safe and memorable adventure.

We accept payments via PayPal and Wise. A 20% deposit is required to confirm your booking.

Since we live on remote islands far from banks or payment centers, and many local services only accept cash, the remaining payment must be made in cash either upon arrival or on the boat. This ensures we can cover all local expenses safely and smoothly.

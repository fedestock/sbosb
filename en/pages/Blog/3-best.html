Best Sailing Boat Tours in San Blas

Prices, Routes & What to Expect

A clear and honest overview...

San Blas is one of the most beautiful sailing destinations in the world — 365 islands, crystal-clear lagoons, coral reefs and remote anchorages that can only be reached by sailboat. But travelers often get confused between different boats, routes and price options.

This guide breaks down the most popular sailboat tours in San Blas for 2025, including prices, typical itineraries, comfort levels, and who each option is ideal for. Whether you’re planning a romantic getaway or an adventure with family or friends, here’s what you need to know.

Why Choose a Sailboat Tour in San Blas

Sailboats offer a more authentic, traditional sailing experience with a cozy atmosphere that lets you explore the San Blas archipelago intimately and at your own pace. These boats typically accommodate small groups (often max 4 guests), allowing for quiet anchorages and access to shallow bays.

Types of Sailboat Tours Available

1
Private Sailboat Charter

This is best if you want total privacy and a personalized itinerary — ideal for couples, honeymooners, or families who want exclusive use of the boat. Private charters usually include your own cabin, all meals, snorkeling gear, and island hopping activities.

Typical price: USD 600–800 per night (entire boat).

2
Private Cabin on a Shared Sailboat

For travelers who want comfort but a slightly lower cost, a private cabin aboard a shared sailboat is a great option. You still get your own space and amenities, but share the boat with a small group.

Typical prices: Private cabin with shared bathroom USD 380; ensuite USD 450.

3
Sailing + Jungle Combination Tour

This popular combo adds a cultural and adventure element to your San Blas sailing experience. In addition to anchorages, snorkeling and beaches, this option includes guided jungle hikes and traditional local experiences.

Typical price: USD 450–600 per night (3-night minimum).

Popular Itineraries

(2–5 nights)

Sailboat tours can vary in length, but the most common itineraries include:

  • 2 Nights — Essential San Blas Escape: Island hopping, snorkeling, sunset sails.
  • 3 Nights — Dutch Cays & Hidden Islands: More time to explore remote anchorages.
  • 4–5 Nights — Deep Exploration: Slow sailing and deep dives into local culture and secluded coves.

What to Expect on Your Tour

Most sailboat options include:

  • All meals prepared on board.
  • Snorkeling equipment and water activities.
  • Island hopping and beach stops.
  • Professional captain and crew.

The best time to sail is generally during the dry season (December to April), when waters are calm and visibility for snorkeling is ideal — but protected anchorages make sailing possible year-round.

"Sailboat tours in San Blas offer a balance of adventure, comfort and authentic exploration — perfect for couples, small groups and nature lovers alike."

Whether you choose a private charter or a shared cabin, San Blas sailing tours provide a memorable way to discover the beauty and solitude of this unique Caribbean destination. Planning ahead and understanding the options makes it easier to find the best experience for your style and budget.

Welcome to San Blas on Sailboats

With Lady Lou, our 43-foot Beneteau, we invite you to discover the San Blas islands through eco-friendly sailing, authentic adventure, and the comfort of a floating home.

Meet the captain

Guadalupe Guirado

Meet the Captain

Founder | Sailor | Explorer

I’m Guadalupe, sailor and explorer. In 2016, I left Argentina on a bicycle with nothing but a backpack and a dream. That journey led me to the sea, and in 2019 I made San Blas my home. Since then, I’ve been sharing my passion for sailing, learning from the Kuna culture, and creating experiences for travelers seeking something real.
